Six services, one studio

From the first sketch to the last planting, the same team designs and builds your garden, so nothing is lost between drawing and ground.

Garden design

Site survey, concept drawings and planting plans shaped around your home, soil and aspect.

Planting

Layered borders and meadow schemes chosen for structure, scent and seasonal change.

Lawn renovation

Levelling, drainage and re-seeding or turfing to bring tired lawns back to a clean, even finish.

Patios and paving

Natural stone terraces, steps and paths, laid with precise levels and tidy joints.

Outdoor living

Dining areas, fire features, lighting and screening that extend the house into the garden.

Maintenance

Seasonal care for new and established gardens, from pruning to feeding and clean-ups.

Planned carefully, built to be lived in

Design before digging
Every project starts with a drawn plan, so you see the garden before any ground is broken.
Close attention to materials
We choose stone, timber and plants to age well, and we check levels and joints as we build.
Clear communication
You get a written estimate, a schedule and one point of contact from first call to handover.
Realistic planning
We talk through budget, access and seasons early, so the plan matches what can be built.

How a project unfolds

  1. Inquiry

    Tell us about your space and what you'd like from it. We reply within a few working days.

  2. Consultation

    We visit the site, listen, and take measurements and notes.

  3. Estimate

    You receive a written estimate with scope, materials and a proposed timeline.

  4. Planning

    We develop the design, agree the details with you and book the work.

  5. Build

    Our team builds the garden, keeps you updated, and walks you through it at handover.
